# notes for devel (0.12.0) version

## In vanilla neovim

### To copy to the system clipboard:

#### In normal mode:

`"+yy` (or whatever the motion is to yank what you need)

#### In visual mode:

It's a bit confusing because here you first make your visual selection
and THEN do `"+y`.

Both of these work when ssh'd to remote machines as well.
